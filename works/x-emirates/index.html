The brief

X Emirates Online was taking orders through WhatsApp messages. That works until it doesn't: there is no funnel to measure, no way to attribute a sale to an ad, and every order costs somebody's time. Their previous performance marketing agency was running ads against that setup and not producing results worth the spend.

01

From WhatsApp Messages to a Shopify Store

We consulted with the team before touching a campaign. Selling in a WhatsApp inbox means no tracked funnel, no attribution, and a ceiling set by how fast a human can reply. Our first recommendation was structural: change the sales channel. Orders moved off WhatsApp messages and onto a Shopify website, where product pages, cart and checkout do the selling and every step is measurable.

02

₹76 Lakhs in Revenue

With the sale happening on the website, growth became something we could build on. The store has done ₹76,65,516 in total sales on Shopify this year, from 1 January to 2 October 2026.

03

8.32x ROAS in July

We rebuilt the ad account from the previous agency: campaign structure, audiences and creative testing run as an ongoing system tied to a real checkout. In July 2026 the account spent ₹1,80,812 and returned ₹15,04,356 in revenue, a purchase ROAS of 8.32x.

04

A Higher Average Order Value

More orders was half the job; we also made each order worth more. We implemented upsells, “buy more, get more value” offers such as the Buy More Save More tiers on product pages (buy 2, 3 or 5 for a bigger discount), combo bundles, and cart upsells, so customers are offered the next sensible product at the moment they are ready to buy.

05

A 9% Conversion Rate, Built Month by Month

There was no single redesign that got the store there. We made consistent improvements to the website every month, testing and refining product pages, offers and the checkout path, until the store was converting at 9%. In June 2026 the store converted 9.39% of online store sessions into sales, up 22% on May.

06

WhatsApp Marketing That Recovers Carts

WhatsApp went from being the checkout to being a marketing channel. We implemented WhatsApp abandoned-cart recovery, messaging shoppers who leave without paying, and 10% of those abandoned carts now come back as placed orders.

07

Higher Rankings on Google

Alongside paid traffic, consistent SEO work with us has moved X Emirates Online to higher rankings on Google, building a source of customers the brand does not pay for per click.

The outcome

Sales moved from WhatsApp messages onto a Shopify store that has done ₹76 lakhs in revenue, with ads returning 8.32x in July, a 9% conversion rate, higher order values, recovered carts and growing search rankings. That is the difference between running ads and running a funnel you can measure and improve.
