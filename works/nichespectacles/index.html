Niche Spectacles

A UK eyewear store carrying vintage designer frames alongside its own contemporary lines, with Shop Pay checkout and UK carriers, plus ongoing SEO.

nichespectacles.com ↗

The brief

Niche Spectacles carries vintage designer frames, Carrera and Dior among them, next to its own contemporary ranges. Two very different kinds of product in one catalog, each with a different buyer. UK customers also expect familiar payment, familiar couriers, and a free-shipping threshold that reads naturally in pounds.

01

Built for the UK, End to End

Everything a UK shopper touches is set up for the UK market: prices in pounds, UK shipping and returns policies, delivery through UK carriers, and a payment gateway configured for UK cards and wallets. Free shipping kicks in on orders over £100, and the cart shows a progress bar so customers can see how close they are, and when they have unlocked it.

02

Mobile Optimised

Most eyewear browsing happens on a phone, so the store was designed to look great on mobile first: a full-screen hero, swipeable collection cards for Contemporary, Vintage and Sunglasses, fast product search, and a thumb-friendly bottom navigation bar for home, search, collections and cart.

03

Apple Pay and Shop Pay for UK Customers

UK shoppers expect one-tap checkout. We implemented Apple Pay and Shop Pay alongside Google Pay and the major cards, so a customer can go from product page to paid order in seconds, without typing in card or address details.

04

A Special Dark Mode

The store includes a dark mode that customers switch on with the sun and moon toggle in the header. The whole store, from the header and collections to the product grid, flips to a dark theme. Frames and lenses stand out against the dark background, and it makes evening browsing easier on the eyes, a small detail that makes the store feel premium.

05

Premium Designer Brands

The catalog brings together a variety of premium designer eyewear, from vintage Carrera Porsche Design frames to Dior, Ferrari and Versace, presented so each brand gets the attention its customers expect.

Carrera

The outcome

A UK store where the vintage and contemporary ranges each have room to sell: priced, delivered and paid for the way UK customers expect, with Apple Pay and Shop Pay checkout, a mobile-first design with dark mode, and SEO running as an ongoing engagement.
