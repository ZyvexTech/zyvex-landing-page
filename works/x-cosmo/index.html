The brief

X Cosmo is the official partner store for three skincare brands: X Emirates, Bluu and Mamamiac. Between them they cover face creams, face washes, serums, sunscreens, body care, hair care and an at-home de-tan kit, for men and for women. The brief was one store where a customer can shop any of the three brands, find the right product for their skin, and check out once, without the three brands blurring into one anonymous catalogue.

01

Three Brands, One Store

The first thing below the hero is Shop by Brand: one tile each for X Emirates, Bluu and Mamamiac, using each brand’s own product photography so a returning customer recognises their brand at a glance. Each tile opens that brand’s own collection.

Behind the tiles it is still one store. A customer can put an X Emirates night cream, a Bluu body lotion and a Mamamiac face wash in the same cart and pay once, which is the whole point of buying from a partner store instead of three separate brand sites.

02

Brand Pages Built for Browsing

Every brand page works the way a skincare shopper browses. A product count up top, a filter for availability, sorting by best selling, name, newest or oldest, and a switch between grid and list views, so a customer can scan a whole range or read each product in detail.

Alongside the brand collections, the catalogue is also organised by what people come looking for: Best Sellers, Combo Packs, Face Wash, Serum, and dedicated Men and Women collections.

03

Made for Skincare Lovers

Skincare buyers shop by concern and by skin, not only by brand, so the store speaks to that from the first screen. The hero slideshow opens with “Get the Glow”: complete skincare for men and women for pimples, dark spots and sun tan. It then splits into a men’s glow regime (Bluu Regime Men, e-Radiance cream) and a women’s glow essence (Bluu Essence Women, e-Luminance cream), each with its own Shop Now.

Further down, “Why Men love this” and “Why Women love this” explain the ingredients in plain language, such as licorice extract for balancing and niacinamide for oil control and smoother texture, each card with its own Add to Cart. The home page FAQ points people to the right range for their skin: Crème X for dullness and uneven tone, Mamamiac Green Tea for oily and acne-prone skin, Rice Water for dry and combination skin.

04

Product Pages That Answer the Questions Skincare Buyers Ask

Skincare is bought on trust in what is inside the jar, so every product page is built to answer that before the customer has to ask. Each one carries the benefits, the key ingredients and what each one does, the full ingredient list as printed on the pack, step-by-step directions for use, a product-specific FAQ and the manufacturer details.

On CXE Crème X, for example, the page explains glutathione, kojic acid, alpha arbutin and vitamin C, gives a four-step night routine, and answers questions like whether it contains bleach and whether it can be used with sunscreen. “You May Also Like” then suggests the next product in the routine.

05

Value Packs and Combos

Skincare is a routine, and routines run out. Product pages offer packs of 1, 2, 3 or 5 right above Add to Cart, so stocking up takes one tap rather than changing the quantity and hoping for a deal.

Combo Packs pair products that work together: Crème X with the salicylic serum, the Vitamin C sunscreen, the Bright Pro face wash or the body lotion, plus two and three packs of the Bluu men’s and women’s creams. Each combo has its own page and its own Add to Cart, which lifts the value of each order without a separate upsell app.

06

Real Customer Results, Front and Centre

For skincare, a real face beats any product shot. The home page and the product pages carry Real Customer Results: a swipeable row of short video reviews from customers, each linked to the product they are using.

A Before & After section follows with customer transformations and their own words, again with Add to Cart under each one, so the proof and the purchase sit side by side.

07

Built for the Phone First

Most skincare shopping happens on a phone, so every page was designed for mobile rather than shrunk down from desktop. On a phone the header becomes a compact bar with the menu, logo, search, wishlist and cart always in reach, and the full menu slides out from the side.

The Shop by Brand tiles stack full width so each brand gets the whole screen. Bestsellers, combos and customer videos become swipeable carousels. On product pages the pack options, quantity and Add to Cart sit within thumb reach, ingredients and directions read comfortably without zooming, and the FAQs fold into tap-to-open answers. Swipe through the screens below.

08

Trust at Every Step

Buying skincare online means trusting the store, so the reassurances are built into the pages rather than hidden in a policy page. A strip on the home page covers free shipping on prepaid orders, exchanges within 30 days and online support around the clock. Product pages show Cash on Delivery availability and an estimated delivery date for the customer’s pincode before they add to cart, and any product can be saved to a wishlist for later.

The home page FAQ answers the questions a first-time customer actually has: which brands they can buy, whether the products are genuine, which product suits their skin type, whether men and women can both use them, how soon to expect results and whether there are side effects. A first-order code and a newsletter sign-up give new visitors a reason to buy now.

The outcome

One Shopify store for three skincare brands. A customer can shop by brand or by need, read exactly what is in a product before buying it, see real results from real customers, stock up with packs and combos, and check out once, comfortably on a phone.
