Before You Ask

Common questions.

How long does a Shopify build take?

It depends on the project scope, but most builds take 3 to 4 weeks from consultation to launch. We confirm the timeline before you commit.

How much does a Shopify build cost?

It depends completely on the scope, since every build is customised. Builds start at ₹25,000 and can go up to ₹1 to 3 lakh depending on the work involved. You get a clear quote after the consultation.

Can you migrate us from another platform?

Yes. We migrate stores to Shopify from other platforms and custom sites, moving your products, customers and orders, with redirects in place to protect the rankings you have already earned.

Do you build custom projects beyond Shopify themes?

Yes. Alongside Shopify themes and Liquid, we build custom projects in React, from custom storefronts to web apps and tools.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. After launch we offer ongoing support for fixes, updates and improvements. You also get full documentation, so your team is never locked in.

Do you work on Shopify Plus projects?

Yes. We build and manage Shopify Plus stores, and we will tell you honestly whether you need Plus or whether standard Shopify is enough.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with brands across India, the UAE, Qatar, the UK and Europe, and run projects remotely across time zones.

Can you handle the marketing too?

Yes. Meta Ads, SEO, CRO and retention, as a monthly retainer. Many clients build with us first and add marketing once the store is live. See the retainer ↗
