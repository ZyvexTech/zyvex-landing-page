Before You Ask

Common questions.

Why is there a minimum monthly ad spend?

Testing needs volume to produce a real answer. Below ₹25,000 a month in ad spend there are not enough conversions to tell a winning campaign from a lucky one, so you would be paying a retainer for guesswork. We would rather tell you that now than take the fee and explain it in month three.

Is there a lock-in period?

It runs month to month. The work compounds, so most engagements get more valuable the longer they run, but that should be your reason to stay rather than a contract.

We already have an agency. Can you take over?

Yes, and it is a common starting point. The onboarding audit covers the existing ad account, the store and the tracking setup before anything is changed, so we know what is worth keeping. You keep ownership of the ad account throughout.

How soon do we see results?

Paid and CRO work can move within weeks. SEO compounds and usually shows meaningful movement in two to four months. Anyone promising you ranking results faster than that is selling you something.

What if you decide we do not need all of this?

Then we say so. The consultation comes before the recommendation, and if the honest answer is that you need a better product page rather than a full retainer, that is the answer you get.

Do you produce the ad creative and content?

No. We plan and run the campaigns, the SEO and the store, and we will tell you what the numbers say is working. The photography, video and ad creative come from your side, so this works best if you already have a content team or a system for producing it. If you do not, that is worth solving before a retainer starts.

Where are your clients based?

Across the Gulf, the UK and Europe as well as closer to home. The work is remote by design, so the constraint has never been location. Budgets and scope are quoted in your currency once we have talked.
